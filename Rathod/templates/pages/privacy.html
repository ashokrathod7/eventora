Privacy Policy

At Eventora, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, and date of birth when you create an account or register for events.

2. Payment Information

For paid events, payments are processed securely through Razorpay. We do not store your credit/debit card details on our servers.

3. Use of Information

Your information is used to process registrations, communicate event updates, and improve our services. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including secure server-side authentication, to protect your personal data from unauthorized access.
